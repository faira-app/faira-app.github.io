A little help finding
your next plan.

Browse the guide below, or get in touch. We’re happy to hear about a problem, a suggestion or a local calendar you’d love to see.

How do I find an event?

Choose a location, then explore Day, Week or Year. Combine search, category, time, cost and distance filters to narrow your options. Filters apply together, so try clearing a few if you see fewer results than expected.

How does Planner work?

Tap an event’s bookmark to add it to your wish list in Planner. Create collections, add private notes and mark events as Planned to arrange your own outings. Published times can help identify overlapping plans; listings without a known duration do not produce time-conflict warnings.

Plans and notes stay on this device. Planned is a private status, not an RSVP or a reservation. Faira never contacts organizers or buys tickets for you. Open the original listing yourself to confirm availability. Deleting a collection keeps its events and notes.

How do state packs and the map work?

Washington is included. In Sources → State packs, you can check for optional free state catalogs as they become available. Installing a pack adds public places and calendar sources; it does not fetch an entire state’s events or a year of listings. Other states are still being prepared.

Choose a city or optionally use your current location. Faira selects nearby sources and updates the week or month you open, reusing cached results. Feeds without date-range support update only when you choose that source.

On Discover, open Map and choose a radius. Event pins use published venue coordinates and the current dates and filters. The Libraries layer shows directory locations, which are separate from connected event feeds. Missing coordinates are never guessed. Apple Maps retrieves content for the area you view.

Why is a listing missing or out of date?

Coverage depends on connected publishers and the schedules they provide. Some calendars supply only part of a schedule. Year counts reflect events already available in the app; an unloaded month does not mean there are no events. Choose the week or month you want to explore, and check Sources for individual calendar status.

Recent results are reused to reduce unnecessary requests. If a publisher asks the app to pause, its next update may be delayed. The organizer’s original listing is the final source for changes or cancellations.

Do I need to share my location?

No. Location permission is optional, and you can choose a city manually. When allowed, your location is used on your device to calculate distance. Distance is a straight-line estimate, not travel distance. Listings without venue coordinates may be excluded when a radius filter is selected.

What do the price filters include?

Prices use the lowest listed ticket amount when the publisher provides one. Fees and other ticket options may cost more. Unknown prices appear under Any price. A paid event without a listed amount cannot match a price ceiling. Confirm the total with the original organizer.

Who handles tickets, registration and refunds?

The original organizer or ticketing service handles purchases, registration, refunds and event questions. Faira does not sell tickets. Follow the event’s original listing link to contact the organizer.

How do I report a problem?

Email faira.app@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Faira version, and the steps that led to the problem. For a listing issue, include the event title and original listing link if you have them.

Please leave out passwords, payment information and precise personal location. Screenshots are optional; remove any private information before sending them.
