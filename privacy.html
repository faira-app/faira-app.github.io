Here’s how Faira handles information while helping you discover local events.

Effective and last updated: November 13, 2026

About this policy

Faira is provided by Nathaniel Oh. This policy covers the Faira iPhone and iPad app and this support website. Contact faira.app@gmail.com with questions about privacy or your information.

Information on your device

Faira keeps saved events, Planner collections, private notes, corrections to saved listings, private events, planned status, installed state catalogs, cached calendar listings, preferences and any custom feed URL you choose to connect in the app’s local storage. The app does not require an account. It does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking.

The developer does not receive your bookmarks, plans, notes, search text or browsing history from the app. Faira does not operate a server that collects this app data.

Optional location access

If you allow location access, Faira uses your location on your device to select nearby public calendars and calculate the distance to events. Coordinates are used in memory and are not saved by Faira. Your precise location is not sent to the developer or appended to calendar requests. Faira does not track your location in the background.

You can use a manually chosen city without granting location permission. You can change or revoke permission at any time in your device’s Settings.

Calendar requests

Faira requests published calendars over HTTPS to obtain event information. Calendar hosts receive ordinary network information, such as your IP address and the requested calendar and date range. Those independent hosts may keep request logs under their own privacy practices.

Faira does not append your precise location, bookmarks, plans, notes or search text to these requests. If you connect a custom feed, the host you choose receives the request. Do not put passwords or other private credentials in feed URLs.

Optional state packs

Washington’s public-source catalog is included. If you choose to check for or download another state pack, Faira requests catalog files from this GitHub Pages website. GitHub receives ordinary network information, including your IP address and the requested file. The request does not include your location, plans or notes. Packs contain public directory and calendar-source data, not executable code or personal data.

Organizer links and Maps

The optional in-app map uses Apple MapKit. Apple retrieves map content for the area you view, which can reveal that map area to Apple. When you choose Find venue on map, the venue or address search you enter is sent to Apple Maps. When you choose Show routes in a day plan, the selected stop coordinates and travel mode are sent to Apple Maps to calculate directions. These stops can include locations you added to private events. Event titles, private notes and the rest of your Planner are not sent in route requests. Faira does not automatically request a route for every plan. See Apple’s Maps privacy information.

Opening an organizer, ticketing or Maps link takes you to that service. Maps receives the public event venue, address or venue coordinates when you open it. The services you choose to visit apply their own terms and privacy policies.

Your Planner is a private workspace. Saving or marking an event as Planned does not contact an organizer, RSVP, register, reserve a place or buy a ticket. Faira does not process event payments or receive payment-card details. Registration, ticket purchases and refunds are handled by the organizer or ticketing service.

When you contact support

If you email faira.app@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to send, including any attachments. This information is used to answer your request, investigate a reported problem and manage the support conversation. It is not used for advertising or sold.

The support mailbox is hosted by Gmail, which processes messages under Google’s Privacy Policy. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request, maintain relevant support records or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing the same address. Do not send passwords, payment-card information or other sensitive details.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not add analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies or contact forms. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes as part of its hosting service. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for its practices.

Email links open your email app; they do not send a message until you choose to send it.

Your choices and deletion

You can remove saved events, clear individual Planner notes, reset saved corrections to publisher details, delete private events, delete collections and remove optional state packs in the app. Deleting a collection keeps its events and notes; edit individual notes to clear them. You can change location access in device Settings. Deleting Faira removes its local app data, subject to your device’s backup and restore behavior. The developer cannot access or remotely delete information stored only on your device.

For privacy questions or requests about information you sent to support, email faira.app@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when Faira’s practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
